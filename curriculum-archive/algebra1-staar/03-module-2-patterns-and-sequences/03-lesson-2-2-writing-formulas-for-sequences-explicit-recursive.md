## **Why Should You Care About Writing Sequence Formulas?**

Imagine knowing exactly how much money you’ll have after saving for months, predicting the number of views your videos will get, or even forecasting the growth of a business. Writing formulas for sequences allows you to **predict future values without listing every single term**—saving time and making you a math powerhouse!

By the end of this lesson, you’ll be able to **write explicit and recursive formulas for arithmetic and geometric sequences**, helping you unlock real-world patterns and problem-solving strategies.

---

## **Two Ways to Write a Sequence Formula**

There are two main ways to describe a sequence mathematically:

### **(A) Recursive Formulas**

✅ Defines a sequence **based on previous terms**.  
✅ Shows how a sequence builds step by step.

✅ Useful for **writing code** or modeling step-by-step processes.

### **(B) Explicit Formulas**

✅ Directly calculates any term in the sequence.  
✅ Lets you jump straight to the **nth term** without listing previous terms.  
✅ Perfect for quickly predicting future values.

---

## **Writing Recursive Formulas**

A **recursive formula** defines each term based on the previous term.

### **(A) Recursive Formula for Arithmetic Sequences**

$a\_n = a\_{n-1} + d$

Where:

* $a\_n$ = current term
* $a\_{n-1}$ = previous term
* $d$ = common difference

🔹 **Important:** You **must** state the first term separately!

#### **Example 1: Writing a Recursive Formula for an Arithmetic Sequence**

Consider the sequence:

$4, 9, 14, 19, 24, ...$

📌 **Step 1: Identify Values**

* $a\_1 = 4$
* $d = 5$

📌 **Step 2: Write the Formula**

$a\_n = a\_{n-1} + 5, \quad a\_1 = 4$

✅ This means each term is found by **adding 5** to the previous term!

---

### **(B) Recursive Formula for Geometric Sequences**

$a\_n = a\_{n-1} \cdot r$

Where:

* $a\_n$ = current term
* $a\_{n-1}$ = previous term
* $r$ = common ratio

🔹 **Important:** Always include the first term separately!

#### **Example 2: Writing a Recursive Formula for a Geometric Sequence**

Consider the sequence:

$10, 30, 90, 270, 810, ...$

📌 **Step 1: Identify Values**

* $a\_1 = 10$
* $r = 3$

📌 **Step 2: Write the Formula**

$a\_n = a\_{n-1} \cdot 3, \quad a\_1 = 10$

✅ This means each term is found by **multiplying the previous term by 3**!

---

## **Writing Explicit Formulas**

An **explicit formula** allows you to find the value of **any** term $a\_n$ without listing all the previous terms.

### **(A) Explicit Formula for Arithmetic Sequences**

$a\_n = a\_1 + (n - 1)d$

Where:

* $a\_n$ = the term you want to find
* $a\_1$ = first term
* $d$ = common difference
* $n$ = term number

#### **Example 3: Writing an Explicit Formula for an Arithmetic Sequence**

Consider the sequence:

$5, 8, 11, 14, 17, ...$

📌 **Step 1: Identify Values**

* $a\_1 = 5$ (first term)
* $d = 3$ (each term increases by 3)

📌 **Step 2: Write the Formula**

$a\_n = 5 + (n - 1)(3)$

📌 **Step 3: Simplify**

$a\_n = 5 + 3n - 3$ $a\_n = 3n + 2$

🔹 **Final Answer:** $a\_n = 3n + 2$  
This formula lets you calculate **any** term without listing every number!

✅ **Find the 50th term:**

$a\_{50} = 3(50) + 2 = 152$

---

### **(B) Explicit Formula for Geometric Sequences**

$a\_n = a\_1 \cdot r^{(n-1)}$

Where:

* $a\_n$ = the term you want to find
* $a\_1$ = first term
* $r$ = common ratio
* $n$ = term number

#### **Example 4: Writing an Explicit Formula for a Geometric Sequence**

Consider the sequence:

$3, 6, 12, 24, 48, ...$

📌 **Step 1: Identify Values**

* $a\_1 = 3$ (first term)
* $r = 2$ (each term is multiplied by 2)

📌 **Step 2: Write the Formula**

$a\_n = 3 \cdot 2^{(n-1)}$

✅ **Find the 6th term:**

$a\_6 = 3 \cdot 2^{(6-1)}$ $a\_6 = 3 \cdot 2^5 = 3 \cdot 32 = 96$
