## **Why Should You Care About Sequences?**

Ever noticed patterns in music beats, the way dominoes fall, or how your social media followers grow over time? These patterns follow **sequences**, which are everywhere—from finance and science to coding and sports!

By the end of this lesson, you’ll be able to **recognize arithmetic and geometric sequences, find common differences and ratios, and predict future terms in a sequence**—skills that will help you understand patterns in real life!

---

## **What Are Sequences?**

A **sequence** is just an **ordered list of numbers** that follows a specific pattern. Each number in the sequence is called a **term**.

Example: **2, 4, 6, 8, 10, ...**  
📌 **Do you see a pattern?** This sequence adds **2** each time.

Sequences are classified into **two main types**:

1. **Arithmetic Sequences** (adding/subtracting the same number)
2. **Geometric Sequences** (multiplying/dividing by the same number)

---

## **Arithmetic Sequences (Adding/Subtracting)**

An **arithmetic sequence** is a sequence where the same number is **added** or **subtracted** each time.

### **Example:**

$3, 7, 11, 15, 19, ...$

📌 **What’s happening?** Each term increases by **4**.

The number that’s added (or subtracted) each time is called the **common difference**, represented by **$d$**.

👉 **Common Difference Formula:**

$d = \text{Next Term} - \text{Previous Term}$

For our example:

$d = 7 - 3 = 4$

🔑 **Key Tip:** If $d$ is **positive**, the sequence increases. If $d$ is **negative**, the sequence decreases.

---

## **Finding the nth Term of an Arithmetic Sequence**

If you want to find **any term** in an arithmetic sequence without listing every number, use this formula:

$a\_n = a\_1 + (n - 1)d$

Where:

* $a\_n$ = the term you want to find
* $a\_1$ = the **first term**
* $d$ = common difference
* $n$ = term number

### **Example:**

Find the **10th term** of the sequence **5, 9, 13, 17, …**

**Step 1:** Identify values:

* $a\_1 = 5$
* $d = 4$
* $n = 10$

**Step 2:** Use the formula:

$a\_{10} = 5 + (10 - 1)(4)$ $a\_{10} = 5 + (9)(4)$ $a\_{10} = 5 + 36 = 41$

📌 **Answer:** The 10th term is **41**!

---

## **Geometric Sequences (Multiplying/Dividing)**

A **geometric sequence** is a sequence where each term is multiplied or divided by the same number.

### **Example:**

$2, 6, 18, 54, 162, ...$

📌 **What’s happening?** Each term is multiplied by **3**.

The number that’s multiplied each time is called the **common ratio**, represented by **$r$**.

👉 **Common Ratio Formula:**

$r = \frac{\text{Next Term}}{\text{Previous Term}}$

For our example:

$r = \frac{6}{2} = 3$

🔑 **Key Tip:** If $r$ is greater than 1, the sequence grows. If $r$ is between 0 and 1, the sequence shrinks.

---

## **Finding the nth Term of a Geometric Sequence**

If you want to find **any term** in a geometric sequence, use this formula:

$a\_n = a\_1 \cdot r^{(n - 1)}$

Where:

* $a\_n$ = the term you want to find
* $a\_1$ = the **first term**
* $r$ = common ratio
* $n$ = term number

### **Example:**

Find the **8th term** of the sequence **3, 9, 27, 81, …**

**Step 1:** Identify values:

* $a\_1 = 3$
* $r = 3$
* $n = 8$

**Step 2:** Use the formula:

$a\_8 = 3 \cdot 3^{(8 - 1)}$ $a\_8 = 3 \cdot 3^7$ $a\_8 = 3 \cdot 2187 = 6561$

📌 **Answer:** The 8th term is **6561**!

## **Real-World Applications of Sequences**

### **(A) Saving Money 💰 (Arithmetic Sequences)**

If you save **$20 every week**, your savings follow an **arithmetic sequence**:

$20, 40, 60, 80, ...$

Using the formula, you can predict how much you’ll have after **10 weeks**!

---

### **(B) Bacteria Growth 🦠 (Geometric Sequences)**

A bacteria culture **doubles every hour**. If you start with **5 bacteria**, the growth follows a **geometric sequence**:

$5, 10, 20, 40, 80, ...$

After **6 hours**, how many bacteria will there be? Use the formula!
