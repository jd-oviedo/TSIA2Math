## **Patterns and Sequences**

Welcome to **Module 2: Patterns and Sequences**—where numbers come alive through predictable patterns!

Have you ever noticed how some things follow a rhythm or a pattern? Whether it’s the beats in a song, the steps in a dance, or even the way your allowance grows each week, sequences are all around us. In this module, you’ll dive into the world of number patterns, learn how to spot them, and write rules to describe them.

This module builds your skills in identifying two major types of sequences—arithmetic and geometric—and helps you express them using explicit and recursive formulas. You’ll discover how sequences connect to real-world situations like saving money, earning interest, or following step-by-step routines.

---

### **What You’ll Learn in This Module:**

#### **Lesson 2.1: Identifying Arithmetic and Geometric Sequences**

* Recognize the difference between arithmetic sequences (adding or subtracting a fixed number) and geometric sequences (multiplying by a fixed number).
* Describe how a sequence is changing over time.
* Use vocabulary like common difference and common ratio to explain the pattern.
* Identify sequences in real-life scenarios.

#### **Lesson 2.2: Writing Formulas for Sequences (Explicit and Recursive)**

* Learn how to write explicit formulas, which help you find any term in a sequence directly.
* Understand recursive formulas, which build each term based on the one(s) before it.
* Translate patterns from a list of numbers into a formula.
* Use formulas to solve problems and make predictions.

---

### **Why This Module Matters:**

Patterns and sequences are a key part of algebra—and life! This module lays the groundwork for understanding more complex functions and equations down the road. By the end of Module 2, you’ll be able to identify and represent sequences, and explain how they grow or change over time.

---

### **Tips for Success:**

* Use color to track how numbers are changing in a sequence.
* Look closely at how each term relates to the one before it—this often gives you the rule!
* Double-check your formulas by plugging in values and seeing if they match the sequence.
* Remember: arithmetic = add or subtract, geometric = multiply or divide.

Let’s explore the power of patterns—because once you can spot them, you can understand and predict so much more!
