## **Systems of Linear Equations and Inequalities**

Welcome to **Module 5: Systems of Linear Equations and Inequalities**—where multiple equations and inequalities come together to help us solve more complex and realistic problems. This module will challenge you to combine everything you’ve learned about linear equations and take it to the next level.

By the end of this module, you’ll be able to solve systems using different strategies, interpret what the solutions mean, and graph both equations and inequalities to represent real-world situations.

---

### **What You’ll Learn in This Module:**

#### **Lesson 5.1: Solving Systems Algebraically – Substitution and Elimination**

* Learn how to solve systems of two linear equations using substitution and elimination methods.
* Understand when and why each method is useful.
* Recognize when systems have one solution, no solution, or infinitely many solutions.
* Practice showing all your steps and checking your answers.

#### **Lesson 5.2: Solving Systems Graphically**

* Graph two linear equations on the same coordinate plane.
* Identify the point of intersection as the solution to the system.
* Analyze whether the lines are parallel, intersecting, or the same line.
* Connect graphs to real-world meaning: what does the solution point actually tell you?

#### **Lesson 5.3: Applications of Systems of Equations**

* Use systems of equations to solve real-world problems involving money, time, distance, and more.
* Turn word problems into math problems—and back again!
* Interpret what your answers mean and explain your thinking clearly.
* Use organized strategies like tables, highlighting, and variable labels to stay focused.

#### **Lesson 5.4: Solving and Graphing Linear Inequalities**

* Solve and graph one-variable linear inequalities.
* Learn when to flip the inequality symbol (hint: it has to do with multiplying or dividing by negatives!).
* Represent solutions on a number line and check your answers with test points.

#### **Lesson 5.5: Graphing Systems of Inequalities**

* Graph two or more inequalities on the same coordinate plane.
* Identify and shade the solution region where all inequalities overlap.
* Analyze systems of inequalities in real-world contexts like budgeting, constraints, and limits.
* Use your graph to determine which points are possible solutions.

---

### **Why This Module Matters:**

Systems of equations and inequalities model situations where multiple things are happening at the same time—like comparing prices at different stores, figuring out when two cars meet, or working within time or budget limits. These are the tools adults use to make real decisions in everyday life!

---

### **Tips for Success:**

* Organize your work. Label your equations and clearly show each step.
* Know when to graph and when to solve algebraically—it’s all about the situation!
* For word problems, slow down and translate carefully.
* When graphing inequalities, don’t forget:

+ Use dashed lines for < or >.
+ Use solid lines for ≤ or ≥.
+ Shade above or below depending on the inequality.

* Always check your solution—does it make sense in the real-world context?

---
