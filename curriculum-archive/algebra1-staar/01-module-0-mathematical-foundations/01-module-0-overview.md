## **Mathematical Foundations**

Before we dive into the exciting world of functions, equations, and graphs, it’s important to make sure your math toolbox is fully stocked! **Module 0: Mathematical Foundations** is all about reviewing the essential skills you need to succeed in Algebra 1. Think of this module as your warm-up lap—it sets the tone and gives you the confidence to handle more complex topics later on.

In this module, you’ll revisit the basics—like how to work with numbers, follow the correct order when solving expressions, apply math properties, and simplify expressions like a pro. These are the must-know skills that make the rest of the course much easier to understand.

---

### **What You’ll Learn in This Module:**

#### **Lesson 0.1: Introduction to Algebra**

* Understand what algebra is and how it builds on what you’ve already learned in math.
* Learn the language of algebra—variables, expressions, equations, and more.
* Discover how algebra helps us represent real-world situations using symbols and rules.

#### **Lesson 0.2: Order of Operations**

* Master the correct order for solving math expressions using PEMDAS:

+ Parentheses
+ Exponents
+ Multiplication & Division (left to right)
+ Addition & Subtraction (left to right)

* Avoid common mistakes and understand why following the order is so important.
* Practice with numeric and algebraic expressions to build fluency.

#### **Lesson 0.3: Properties of Operations**

* Learn and apply key properties:

+ Commutative Property
+ Associative Property
+ Distributive Property
+ Identity and Inverse Properties

* Use these properties to rewrite and simplify expressions effectively.
* Understand how these rules work behind the scenes in every math problem you solve.

#### **Lesson 0.4: Simplifying & Evaluating Expressions**

* Simplify algebraic expressions by combining like terms and applying the distributive property.
* Evaluate expressions by plugging in values for variables.
* Develop the habits of careful substitution and double-checking your work.
* Start connecting numerical skills to algebraic thinking.

---

### **Why This Module Matters:**

Math is like building a house—you need a strong foundation first. These early skills might seem familiar, but they’re essential for solving equations, graphing, and everything else coming up. If you’re solid here, the rest of Algebra 1 will make a lot more sense and feel a lot less stressful.

---

### **Tips for Success:**

* Don’t rush through this module—even if it feels like review, it’s the key to your success.
* Use a color-coded system to highlight parts of expressions (like terms, operations, and variables).
* Always follow the order of operations—even when there are variables!
* Think of math properties like shortcuts—they help simplify problems and keep your work organized.

---

**By the end of this module, you’ll be confident with the math basics and ready to jump into more advanced algebra topics. Let’s get started with Lesson 0.1!**
