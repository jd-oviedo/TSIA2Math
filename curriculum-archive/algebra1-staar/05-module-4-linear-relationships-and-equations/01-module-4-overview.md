## **Linear Relationships and Equations**

Welcome to **Module 4: Linear Relationships and Equations**—one of the most important parts of Algebra 1! In this module, you’ll explore how linear equations help us model and solve real-world problems. You’ll dive deep into lines, slopes, intercepts, and how equations can represent movement, change, and patterns in everyday life.

By the end of this module, you’ll be able to write, solve, and graph linear equations like a math pro—and interpret what your graphs actually mean in real-world situations.

---

### **What You’ll Learn in This Module:**

#### **Lesson 4.1: Direct Variation**

* Understand what direct variation is and how it’s a special type of linear relationship.
* Explore equations in the form ![LaTeX: y=kx](https://k12.instructure.com/equation_images/y%253Dkx?scale=1 "y=kx"), where k is the constant of variation.
* Learn how to tell when two variables change at a constant rate through the origin.

#### **Lesson 4.2: Understanding Linear Equations (Intro to Slope and Forms)**

* Explore what slope means and how it shows change.
* Get introduced to different forms of linear equations:

+ Slope-intercept form (![LaTeX: y=mx+b](https://k12.instructure.com/equation_images/y%253Dmx%252Bb?scale=1 "y=mx+b"))
+ Point-slope form
+ Standard form

* Learn how to identify slope and y-intercept from equations.

#### **Lesson 4.3: Solving Linear Equations**

* Solve one-variable linear equations using inverse operations.
* Practice combining like terms, using distributive property, and isolating the variable.
* Apply equation-solving skills to real-world and multi-step problems.

#### **Lesson 4.4: Writing and Graphing Linear Equations**

* Write equations from graphs, tables, and verbal descriptions.
* Graph linear equations using slope and intercept.
* Connect algebraic equations to their visual meaning on a graph.

#### **Lesson 4.5: Slope as a Rate of Change**

* Interpret slope as a rate of change in real-world situations (like speed, cost, or temperature).
* Understand units of slope and what they mean in context.
* Practice identifying and calculating slope from different representations.

#### **Lesson 4.6: Graphing Linear Functions and Key Features**

* Learn how to graph linear functions and analyze their features:

+ x- and y-intercepts
+ Increasing or decreasing behavior
+ Constant rate of change

* Use graphs to interpret and describe real-life scenarios.

#### **Lesson 4.7: Transformations of Linear Functions**

* Explore how changing slope and y-intercept affects the graph of a line.
* Understand how lines move (translate), stretch, or reflect based on changes to the equation.
* Predict and describe transformations before graphing.

#### **Lesson 4.8: Parallel and Perpendicular Lines**

* Discover how slopes can show relationships between lines:

+ Parallel lines have equal slopes.
+ Perpendicular lines have opposite reciprocal slopes.

* Use slopes and equations to identify and write equations of lines that are parallel or perpendicular.

---

### **Why This Module Matters:**

Linear equations are everywhere—business, science, sports, travel—you name it. This module gives you the tools to understand and model how things are changing, growing, or moving in the world around you. Mastering linear relationships opens the door to more advanced algebra and real-life problem-solving.

---

### **Tips for Success:**

* Slope is your friend. Always look for it—it tells you how fast something is changing.
* Use color-coding when graphing to help distinguish lines and key features.
* Ask yourself: What does this graph or equation mean in real life?
* Practice with real-world examples: money earned over time, distance traveled, or number of followers gained.

---

**By the end of Module 4, you’ll be able to confidently create, solve, and analyze linear equations and graphs. You won’t just be doing math—you’ll be using math to understand the world around you. Let’s get started with Lesson 4.1!**
