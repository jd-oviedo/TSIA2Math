## **1. What Are Polynomial Applications?**

* **Physics:** Motion, force, and energy equations

* **Finance:** Revenue and cost models
* **Biology:** Population growth and genetics

## **2. Types of Polynomial Applications**

### **(A) Area and Volume Models**

Polynomials are useful in **geometry**, especially when finding the area and volume of complex shapes.

#### **Example 1: Finding the Area of a Garden**

A rectangular garden has a length of **$x + 4$** meters and a width of **$x - 2$** meters. Write a polynomial expression for the area.

📌 **Step 1: Use the area formula**

$\text{Area} = \text{Length} \times \text{Width}$

📌 **Step 2: Multiply the expressions**

$(x + 4)(x - 2)$

📌 **Step 3: Expand using the distributive property**

$x^2 - 2x + 4x - 8 = x^2 + 2x - 8$

✅ **Final Answer:**

$x^2 + 2x - 8$

This polynomial represents the area of the garden based on different values of $x$.

---

### **(B) Profit and Revenue Models**

Businesses use polynomial functions to model **profit, cost, and revenue**.

#### **Example 2: Maximizing Profit**

A company sells a product at **$x$ dollars per unit**, and the revenue function is given by:

$R(x) = -2x^2 + 100x$

where **$x$** is the number of units sold. Find the maximum revenue.

📌 **Step 1: Identify the polynomial function**

$R(x) = -2x^2 + 100x$

📌 **Step 2: Find the vertex**  
Since this is a **quadratic polynomial ($ax^2 + bx + c$)**, the maximum value occurs at:

$x = -\frac{b}{2a}$

Substituting **$a = -2$** and **$b = 100$**:

$x = -\frac{100}{2(-2)} = \frac{100}{4} = 25$

📌 **Step 3: Find the maximum revenue**

$R(25) = -2(25)^2 + 100(25)$ $= -2(625) + 2500$ $= -1250 + 2500 = 1250$

✅ **Final Answer:**  
The **maximum revenue is $1,250** when selling **25 units**.

---

### **(C) Motion and Physics Applications**

Polynomial equations describe **object motion** in physics.

#### **Example 3: Object in Free Fall**

The height of a ball thrown into the air is modeled by:

$h(t) = -16t^2 + 64t + 80$

where **$h(t)$** is the height (feet) and **$t$** is the time (seconds).

**Find when the ball reaches its maximum height.**

📌 **Step 1: Identify the quadratic polynomial**

$h(t) = -16t^2 + 64t + 80$

📌 **Step 2: Find the vertex**

$t = -\frac{b}{2a} = -\frac{64}{2(-16)} = \frac{64}{32} = 2$

📌 **Step 3: Find the maximum height**

$h(2) = -16(2)^2 + 64(2) + 80$ $= -16(4) + 128 + 80$ $= -64 + 128 + 80 = 144$

✅ **Final Answer:**  
The ball reaches a **maximum height of 144 feet after 2 seconds**.

---

### **(D) Population Growth & Science**

Polynomial models are used to **predict population growth and decay in science and biology**.

#### **Example 4: Modeling Bacteria Growth**

A bacteria population follows the polynomial model:

$P(t) = 3t^3 + 5t^2 + 20$

where **$P(t)$** is the number of bacteria after $t$ hours. Find the population after **4 hours**.

📌 **Step 1: Substitute $t = 4$**

$P(4) = 3(4)^3 + 5(4)^2 + 20$

📌 **Step 2: Simplify**

$P(4) = 3(64) + 5(16) + 20$ $= 192 + 80 + 20 = 292$

✅ **Final Answer:**  
After **4 hours, there are 292 bacteria**.
