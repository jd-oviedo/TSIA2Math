## **Quadratic Functions and Equations**

Welcome to **Module 7: Quadratic Functions and Equations**—where your algebra skills level up! This module introduces you to parabolas, curved graphs that model everything from the arc of a basketball shot to the shape of satellite dishes. You’ll learn to write, graph, transform, and solve quadratic equations, as well as analyze real-world data using quadratic models. By the end of this module, you’ll be a true parabola pro!

---

### **What You’ll Learn in This Module:**

#### **Lesson 7.1: Understanding Quadratic Functions**

* Understand what makes a function **quadratic**.
* Explore the **standard form** of a quadratic:  
  $y = ax^2 + bx + c$
* Learn how quadratic functions are **different from linear functions**.
* Identify the **parabola** and its **opening direction**, based on the value of $a$.

📝 *Helpful Hint:* Remember—if the graph makes a smiley face (opens up), $a$ is positive. If it’s a frown (opens down), $a$ is negative!

---

#### **Lesson 7.2: Writing and Graphing Quadratic Equations**

* Practice writing quadratic equations from different forms (standard, vertex, and factored).
* Graph parabolas by hand using **tables**, **intercepts**, and **the vertex**.
* Use technology (like Desmos) to double-check graphs.

📝 *Helpful Hint:* Use the formula $x = -\frac{b}{2a}$ to find the **axis of symmetry** and vertex quickly when graphing from standard form.

---

#### **Lesson 7.3: Key Features of Quadratic Graphs**

* Identify and describe key features of quadratic graphs:

  + **Vertex**
  + **Axis of symmetry**
  + **Maximum or minimum**
  + **x-intercepts (zeros)**
  + **y-intercept**
  + **Domain and range**
* Understand how each feature connects to the equation.

📝 *Helpful Hint:* Sketch the axis of symmetry first—it helps you make a symmetrical graph more easily!

---

#### **Lesson 7.4: Factors and Zeros**

* Learn how factoring can help you find the **x-intercepts** (aka **zeros or roots**) of a parabola.
* Connect factored form to the graph:  
  $y = (x - r\_1)(x - r\_2)$
* Practice solving by factoring and checking your work by plugging zeros back in.

📝 *Helpful Hint:* The solutions to the equation are where the graph crosses the x-axis. If your equation doesn’t factor easily, try a different method!

---

#### **Lesson 7.5: Transformations of Quadratic Functions**

* Explore how parabolas change when you:

  + Shift them left, right, up, or down
  + Stretch or compress them
  + Reflect them across the x-axis
* Compare transformations in **vertex form**:  
  $y = a(x - h)^2 + k$

📝 *Helpful Hint:* The vertex is $(h, k)$, but watch out for the **minus sign** in the formula! It tricks many students.

---

#### **Lesson 7.6: Solving Quadratic Equations (All Methods)**

* Master the **four ways** to solve quadratics:

  1. **Factoring**
  2. **Graphing**
  3. **Square roots**
  4. **Quadratic formula**
* Learn how to choose the **best method** for each equation type.
* Discover how the **discriminant** helps predict the number of real solutions.

📝 *Helpful Hint:* The quadratic formula is your best friend. Practice it until you can use it with confidence. And don’t forget to simplify your radicals!

---

#### **Lesson 7.7: Quadratic Regressions and Data Modeling**

* Use technology to **model real-world data** with quadratic functions.
* Learn how to run a **quadratic regression** using a calculator or online tool.
* Interpret what the **equation and graph** mean in a real-life context (height, time, area, etc.).

📝 *Helpful Hint:* Pay attention to the **context** of the data. What does the vertex represent? What do the zeros mean in the real world?

---

### **Why This Module Matters:**

Quadratic functions model **real-world motion**, patterns, and change. They’re a key part of physics, engineering, sports, business, and computer graphics. In this module, you’ll develop algebraic skills that are essential for success in future math courses and in everyday problem-solving.

---

### **Tips for Success:**

* Keep a cheat sheet of quadratic formulas and graphing steps handy.
* Get comfortable with graphing calculators or graphing apps like Desmos.
* Check your solutions by plugging them back into the original equation.
* Color-code your notes to highlight key features of the equations and graphs.
* Practice interpreting graphs—don’t just solve equations. Understanding the story behind the graph is just as important!

---

By the end of Module 7, you’ll be able to confidently analyze, write, and solve quadratic equations, interpret graphs, and apply your knowledge to real-world situations. Get ready to take your algebra skills to new heights—parabolas await!
