Imagine being able to **solve for any variable** in a formula, no matter what’s missing. Want to figure out how long a trip will take if you know the distance and speed? Or how to adjust a recipe for fewer people? **Literal equations** help you rearrange formulas so they work for your situation.

Learning to work with literal equations turns you into a flexible problem solver in **math, science, business—even cooking and design!**

## **What Is a Literal Equation?**

A **literal equation** is an equation that has **two or more variables**. Instead of plugging in numbers right away, your goal is to **rearrange the equation** to solve for one variable in terms of the others.

🧠 Think of it like being a math detective—you’re isolating a variable to find out what it equals!

**Examples of literal equations:**

* $A = \pi r^2$ (area of a circle)
* $d = rt$ (distance = rate × time)
* $y = mx + b$ (slope-intercept form)

---

## **Solving a Literal Equation: Step-by-Step**

Let’s say you’re solving for a variable. You’ll follow these same steps each time.

### 📌 Step 1: Identify the variable you need to isolate

Underline or circle it!

### 📌 Step 2: Use inverse operations

Think: “What’s being done to the variable?” Do the opposite to both sides.

### 📌 Step 3: Keep it balanced

Whatever you do to one side, do to the other.

---

### **Example 1: Solving for a Variable in a Formula**

**Formula:** $d = rt$  
Let’s solve for **t** (time)

📌 Step 1: Circle the variable you want → **t**

📌 Step 2: “t” is being multiplied by “r” → divide both sides by **r**

$\frac{d}{r} = \frac{rt}{r}$

📌 Step 3: Simplify

✅ **Final Answer:** $t = \frac{d}{r}$

You just rearranged the formula to solve for time!

---

### **Example 2: Solve for y in Standard Form**

**Formula:** $Ax + By = C$  
Let’s solve for **y**

📌 Step 1: Subtract $Ax$ from both sides

$By = C - Ax$

📌 Step 2: Divide both sides by **B**

$y = \frac{C - Ax}{B}$

✅ Now it's in slope-intercept form!

---

## **Real-World Formulas You Might See**

### (A) Area of a Triangle

**Formula:** $A = \frac{1}{2}bh$  
Solve for **b** (base)

📌 Multiply both sides by 2:

$2A = bh$

📌 Divide by **h**:

$b = \frac{2A}{h}$

---

### (B) Slope Formula

**Formula:** $m = \frac{y\_2 - y\_1}{x\_2 - x\_1}$  
Solve for $y\_2$

📌 Multiply both sides by $(x\_2 - x\_1)$:

$m(x\_2 - x\_1) = y\_2 - y\_1$

📌 Add $y\_1$ to both sides:

$y\_2 = m(x\_2 - x\_1) + y\_1$

## **Helpful Tips for Success**

✅ **Always ask: “What’s being done to my variable?”** Undo it!  
✅ Treat variables just like numbers—don’t let the letters scare you.  
✅ Work step-by-step and keep your equation balanced.  
✅ Double-check that your variable is by itself at the end.  
✅ Look for common formulas in math and science classes—they often pop up again!
