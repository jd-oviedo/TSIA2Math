#### **Part 1: Guided Notes**

##### The Denominator Is a Unit, Not a Number to Combine

When you add $3$ apples and $5$ apples you get $8$ apples. You do not get $8$ apples squared, and you do not add the word "apples" to itself.

Fractions work the same way. The denominator names **what kind of thing** you are counting.

$$\frac{3}{x} + \frac{5}{x} = \frac{8}{x}$$

Three of them plus five of them is eight of them. The $x$ says what "them" means, and it comes along unchanged.

**The denominator is not added. It is not multiplied. It is kept.**

That is the single most important sentence in this topic, and the two commonest wrong answers both break it:

$$\frac{3}{x} + \frac{5}{x} \neq \frac{8}{2x} \quad\text{and}\quad \frac{3}{x} + \frac{5}{x} \neq \frac{8}{x^{2}}$$

Check with numbers. At $x = 1$ the original is $3 + 5 = 8$. The first wrong answer gives $4$, the second gives $8$ but fails at $x = 2$, where the original gives $4$ and it gives $2$.

##### Same Denominator: Combine the Tops

$$\frac{2x}{7} + \frac{3x}{7} = \frac{5x}{7}$$

Add the numerators. Keep the denominator. That is the whole procedure when the bottoms already match.

##### Different Denominators: Rescale First

$$\frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{2x}$$

These count different things, so they cannot be combined yet. Rewrite both over a common denominator.

Step 1: Find a denominator both will fit. $2x$ works, since $x$ divides into it.

Step 2: Rescale each fraction. **Whatever you multiply the bottom by, you must multiply the top by.**

$$\frac{1}{x} = \frac{1 \cdot 2}{x \cdot 2} = \frac{2}{2x}$$

The second fraction already has denominator $2x$, so it does not change.

Step 3: Now the denominators match, so combine the tops.

$$\frac{2}{2x} + \frac{1}{2x} = \frac{3}{2x}$$

Step 4: Check at $x = 1$. The original gives $1 + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2}$, and $\frac{3}{2}$ agrees.

**Rescaling the bottom without rescaling the top is the error to watch here.** Writing $\frac{1}{2x} + \frac{1}{2x} = \frac{2}{2x}$ changes the value of the first fraction, and the answer is wrong before any adding happens.

##### The Mistake That Costs the Most Points

**Applying a subtraction to only the first term of the second numerator.**

$$\frac{x + 3}{5} - \frac{x - 2}{5}$$

The minus sign is in front of the **whole second fraction**, so it applies to everything in that numerator.

Step 1: Write the subtraction across the top, with the second numerator in brackets. This is the step that prevents the error, and it costs one line.

$$\frac{(x + 3) - (x - 2)}{5}$$

Step 2: Distribute the minus. Every sign in the second bracket flips.

$$\frac{x + 3 - x + 2}{5}$$

Notice the $-2$ became $+2$.

Step 3: Combine. $x - x = 0$ and $3 + 2 = 5$.

$$\frac{5}{5} = 1$$

**The wrong version** drops the brackets and subtracts only the $x$:

$$\frac{x + 3 - x - 2}{5} = \frac{1}{5}$$

One is $1$ and the other is $\frac{1}{5}$. Substituting any value at all separates them: at $x = 0$ the original is $\frac{3}{5} - \frac{-2}{5} = \frac{5}{5} = 1$.

**Write the brackets. Every time.** They are not decoration; they are the thing that reminds you the minus has more than one job.

##### Order Matters in a Subtraction

$$\frac{7}{x - 2} - \frac{3}{x - 2} = \frac{4}{x - 2}$$

The first numerator minus the second, in that order. Computing $3 - 7$ gives $-4$, which is the negative of the right answer.

Addition can be done in any order. Subtraction cannot. If you reorder the fractions, the sign of the answer changes.

##### Simplify at the End, If You Can

$$\frac{5}{2x} - \frac{3}{2x} = \frac{2}{2x} = \frac{1}{x}$$

The denominators already matched, so the tops combined to $2$. Then the $2$ on top and the $2$ underneath are common factors, so they cancel.

Simplifying is the last step, never the first. Cancelling before the fractions are combined removes something that was only part of one numerator.

##### When the Denominators Share Nothing

$$\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{x}$$

Nothing divides between $3$ and $x$, so the common denominator is their product, $3x$.

Step 1: Rescale the first. Multiply top and bottom by $x$. $\frac{x}{3x}$.

Step 2: Rescale the second. Multiply top and bottom by $3$. $\frac{3}{3x}$.

Step 3: Combine the tops.

$$\frac{x + 3}{3x}$$

The answer cannot be simplified further, because $x + 3$ is a single term added, not a factor. Nothing cancels against the $3x$.

##### The Five Traps

1. **Adding or multiplying the denominators.** $\frac{3}{x} + \frac{5}{x}$ is $\frac{8}{x}$, not $\frac{8}{2x}$ and not $\frac{8}{x^{2}}$. A shared denominator is kept.
2. **Rescaling the bottom but not the top.** Whatever multiplies the denominator multiplies the numerator too, or the fraction's value changes.
3. **Subtracting only the first term of the second numerator.** Put the second numerator in brackets before you distribute the minus.
4. **Subtracting in the wrong order.** First minus second. Reversing it flips the sign of the answer.
5. **Simplifying too early.** Combine first, cancel last. A factor of one numerator is not a factor of the combined expression.
